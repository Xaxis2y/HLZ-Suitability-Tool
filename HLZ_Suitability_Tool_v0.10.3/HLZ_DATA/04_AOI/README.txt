04_AOI  -  Area of interest  [OPTIONAL]
==============================================================================

WHAT GOES HERE
  ONE polygon layer (shapefile, GeoJSON, GeoPackage or geodatabase feature class).

WHY
  Landing-zone centres are placed only inside it. Without it, the current map view or the whole DTM is used.

TIPS
  - The DTM must extend at least the approach range (about 1 km) beyond the AOI on every side, or approaches toward the edge cannot be checked.
  - Keep only one AOI here; store alternatives in 06_Reference.

Run '0. Set Up and Audit Data Folder' (ArcGIS Pro) or AUDIT_DATA_FOLDER.bat to check this folder.
