03_Exclusions  -  Other exclusion areas  [OPTIONAL]
==============================================================================

WHAT GOES HERE
  Polygon layers (same formats as hydrology).

WHY
  Any other area a landing zone must not touch: wetlands, built-up areas, restricted or no-fly areas, sports fields in use, and so on.

TIPS
  - Polygons only. Every polygon layer here is used as an exclusion layer.

WHERE TO GET IT (check each licence)
  - Ontario Wetlands (Ontario GeoHub)
  - CanVec land-cover and built-up areas
