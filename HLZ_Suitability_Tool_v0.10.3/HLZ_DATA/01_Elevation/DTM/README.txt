01_Elevation\DTM  -  Bare-earth elevation (DTM)  [REQUIRED]
==============================================================================

WHAT GOES HERE
  ONE single-band elevation raster: GeoTIFF (.tif) recommended; also .img, .asc, .vrt, .crf, or a raster or mosaic dataset inside a file geodatabase (.gdb).

WHY
  Ground height with trees and buildings removed. Used for slope, surface roughness and the terrain under every approach path.

TIPS
  - Projected coordinate system in metres (UTM or MTM). Not latitude/longitude and not Web Mercator.
  - 1 m cells or finer are recommended; 2.5 m or coarser is screening quality only.
  - Several tiles? Combine them into ONE raster (Mosaic To New Raster) or a mosaic dataset, and keep only that here. Put the original tiles in 06_Reference.
  - Set a NoData value (often -32767 or -9999) so voids are not read as ground.

WHERE TO GET IT (check each licence)
  - NRCan High Resolution Digital Elevation Model (HRDEM), product 'DTM' (Canada, 1 m / 2 m)
  - Ontario Digital Terrain Model (lidar-derived), Ontario GeoHub
  - USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) 1 m DEM (United States)
