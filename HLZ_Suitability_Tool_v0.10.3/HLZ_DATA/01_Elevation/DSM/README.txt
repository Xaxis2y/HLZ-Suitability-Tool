01_Elevation\DSM  -  Surface model (DSM)  [RECOMMENDED]
==============================================================================

WHAT GOES HERE
  ONE single-band surface raster (same formats as the DTM).

WHY
  Height of the top of everything: trees, buildings, towers. DSM minus DTM is the obstacle height. Without it, obstacles are not detected and every rating is capped at Marginal.

TIPS
  - Same coordinate system, cell size and grid alignment as the DTM is fastest (no resampling).
  - Same vertical datum as the DTM (for example both CGVD2013), otherwise obstacle heights are offset.
  - Must come from the same survey as the DTM where possible.

WHERE TO GET IT (check each licence)
  - NRCan HRDEM, product 'DSM'
  - Ontario lidar-derived Digital Surface Model, Ontario GeoHub

Run '0. Set Up and Audit Data Folder' (ArcGIS Pro) or AUDIT_DATA_FOLDER.bat to check this folder.
